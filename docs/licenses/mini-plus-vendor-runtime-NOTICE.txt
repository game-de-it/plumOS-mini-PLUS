mini PLUS vendor runtime status notice

The exact boot, kernel, firmware, module and Mali files used for GKD
mini PLUS hardware validation were captured from MINIPLUS / BBG v5.3 2024-10-15.
They are not plumOS-authored files and are not stored in this Git repository.
Their identities and capture procedures are recorded in
configs/mini-plus-vendor-artifacts.json.

The project maintainer created and publicly released the plumOS-GKD stockOS
reference and attests that the GKD mini PLUS vendor granted permission to use and
redistribute the stockOS-derived files required by plumOS-mini-PLUS. No separate
written license file was issued. The stockOS distribution and GKD-authored
material remain the property of GKD and are outside the plumOS-mini-PLUS MIT
License. Third-party rights contained in stockOS remain with their respective
owners. See GKD-stockOS-PERMISSION-NOTICE.txt.

The captured Mali userspace reports g13p0-01eac0 while the preserved kernel
reports g2p0-01eac0. This exact pair is retained because it has passed mini PLUS
hardware validation. Substitution with another g13p0 library is not allowed by
the build contract.

An ARM Mali userspace EULA published with a public Rockchip libmali collection
does not identify these exact captures, so it is retained only as a reference.
Publication of the exact GKD stockOS capture instead relies on the vendor
permission attested above. This does not relicense Arm software or override
rights and conditions held by Arm or other third parties.
